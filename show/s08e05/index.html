4 years ago back when this show when still in its teenage years I would mix songs regardless of their musical genre. For the last 4 years I behaved myself and try to keep it genre coherent. None of this with this show where you get rock, rap and electro all thrown together. A great opportunity for me to dig back in my library and basically expose the very own musical mess that's inside my head and guts. A show that's bound to be on repeat on my ipod as should be. A show that you might enjoy if you're a freak like me. Proud to be hosted on Radio 666 and Radio Raptz.
