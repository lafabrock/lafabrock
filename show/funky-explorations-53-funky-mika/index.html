Funky Explorations #53 (Funky Mika)

En ligne le 10 mars 202060 min18 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗Écouter sur hearthis ↗
Gelale ( http://mixcloud.com/Gelale ) and I are pleased to welcome our first Finnish funky dude to the Funky Explorations ! Funky Mika is the type of dude who can't really choose between Funk and Jazz. With a mere 27 shows under his belt, he has gained affection from our sweet Mixcloud community of Funk Lovers with his feverish funk mixes exploring the grooviest and soulful hours of Funk Music. It was only about time that we ask him to contribute to the Funky Explorations. For you he delivers a joyful celebration of punchy funk anthems that we simply loved expanding on. Take your temperature : https://www.mixcloud.com/FunkyMika/ La fabrock peels the ingredients during the first 20 minutes Gelale chops up the the hot stuff for the next 20 minutes Funky Mika boils it down through the last 20 minutes As heard on Radio 666 and Raptz.
