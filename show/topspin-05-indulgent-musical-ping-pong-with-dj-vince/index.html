Topspin #05 - Indulgent Musical Ping-Pong with DJ Vince

En ligne le 26 février 201831 min12 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗Écouter sur hearthis ↗
Les tontons transistors présentent Topspin ! https://www.facebook.com/tontontransistor/ We all love to play (with) music. And musical ping-pongs are a great way to get familiar with the music makers around you. I've been following Vince on Mixcloud for a while so when I started the Topspin series, he was one of the first I thought of as a sparring partner : enthusiast, resourceful, eclectic, skilled, Vince is really the kind of DJ you want to invite for a musical ping-pong ! Be sure to check out his mixes on https://www.mixcloud.com/DjVince/ This 30 min. mix brings you everything from nervous blends of classical music and rap to Jungle madness, from Jamaican vibes to french electronic errands in Brest, from a walk in the clouds to Turkish rockish delicacies, from remixes of Bowie to remixes of Gainsbourg. Le next Tonton à monter sur la table ce sera Johnny avec un invité très spécial et inattendu ! Free Dl Inside (track 12) : https://soundcloud.com/le-gout-du-son
