So good to be back again with new music for your ears! This time with funky soul grooves from all over the world, mostly new releases that we enjoyed over the last few weeks! We don't matter if it's * blues (G Love), * rap (Calibro feat. Illa J), * afro (The Soul Motivators, Antibalas, Hailu Mergia), * psyche rock (King Khan), * dark (Gill Scott gets remixed), * mexican that sings in spanish (Mariachi El Bronx) * organic neon roots (Amambay 100.000) * queer (Lido Pimienta) * old (Camille Yarbrough, Peru Negro) * or new (Tom Misch) ... it's all about the music I love and love to share with you. Enjoy the sounds of La fabrock on Radio 666 and Raptz.
