What up you Funky Explorators ? Ready for the return of your favourite series ? For this new number, Gelale ( https://www.mixcloud.com/Gelale/ ) and I have incomings from Kilkenny Ireland in the name of Ste45 from the beloved 7inch collective. Ste45 and his compadre Johnny 7 are Seven Inch Vinyl lovers, the kind that plays back to back for hours ! Funk, punk, soul, ska and rock'n'roll to big beat, disco or indie, you name it they got it ! For their Funky Explorations they decided to celebrate love and freedom and we couldn't agree more ! Reach out 7inch further : https://www.mixcloud.com/theseveninchcollective/ 20 minutes each : Gelale opens with Japanese and Zombies Next Ste45 brings freedom and love La fabrock keeps you dancing on a funky mule
