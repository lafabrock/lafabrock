Here’s another focus on interesting electro releases late 2016-early 2017 with music by Flaming Lips, Underworld, Armand Van Helden, Cashmere Cat, Nathan Fake , Romare, Clark, Charli XCX, Claude, Bomba Estereo, African Head Charge and more. In this episode : Beats, fucked-up beats, funky beats, symphonic beats, global beats Remixers : Omulu, Danny L Harle Music from such great labels as : Warp, Brainfeeder, Ninja Tunes, PC Music, Musique Large, Moshi Moshi, On-U Sound, [pias], Defected, Edible, Booty Call records, Caroline Fav track this week : Anna Meredith – Nautilius. AM is a Scottish electronic and acoustic music composer that used to be involved with the BBC Scottish Symphony Orchestra. Her first album “Varmints” was released 2016 to critical acclaim.
