This week is the long awaited lafabrock's real rock show featuring my man from Nantes Alex Ruiz from Le Petit Bazar Electro crossing over from radio host to singer songwriter. Expect the same energy from LPBE, and a poetry that only hardcore fans suspected. Check him out on fb : https://www.facebook.com/alexruizchanteur Also, some strong 2020 releases * from Sonic Youth's Kim Gordon, still angry #metoo, * more female rock with newcomers Diet Cig and Shopping, * Take me back to 1995 for more angry Swedish dudes Whale * Johnny Depp X Alice Cooper X Joe Perry cover David Bowie * Algiers, Chicano Batman, Khani El Khatib * Surf rock "can't get enough of Pulp Fiction's Misirlou" by BRUUT! * Italian hardcore by Disco Mostro * headbanging time with DragonForce * bleeding ears with Cattle Decapitation Thank you for tuning in. Stay strong. Share the love. Spread happiness. Enjoy summer. Enjoy the sounds of La fabrock on Radio 666 and Raptz.
