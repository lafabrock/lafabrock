Gelale ( https://www.mixcloud.com/Gelale ) and I are very happy to welcome you to this new episode of our funky explorations. This week, we bring another frenchman to the table, the irresistible, the magnetic, the unpinnable Alex ! You may know him from his sunday evening live eclectic radio show Le Petit Bazar Electro where he talks, scratches and never fails to surprise you with a little help from his friends as curators. Beats, Funk, Rock, World, Electro, Sardines or Peruvians, you never know what you're in store for, but rest asure it's gonna electrify you in the best possible way. Check him out on mixcloud https://www.mixcloud.com/alexandre-ruiz/ or sun http://www.lesonunique.com/. La fabrock feeds the gators for the first 20 minutes Alex brings some girls to the fight through the next 20 minutes Gelale melts hearts and snow until the end !
