La fabrock is happy to bring you funky music from around the world ! In this session, we take you from Cameroon, to Senegal, to Jamaica, from Notting Hill to New Orleans, from China to New-York, to Brasil, to Berlin... We fear no splits and instead thrive on the richeness of world music as it is in 2018. Those are mostly 2018 releases with artists borrowing from James Brown, Sade, Bob Marley, Wu-Tang to bring all the colours of the rainbow to your ears. We're live on radio 666 radio666.com (http://http://radio666.com/) every thursday at 11 pm and Raptz (http://www.raptz.com/) on sundays at 5 pm. Join us on facebook, youtube or tumblr for more fun.
