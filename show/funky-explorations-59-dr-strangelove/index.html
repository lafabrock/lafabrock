Dr Strangelove is one enigmatic dude. Apart from the fact that he likes sailing and digging records his Mixcloud profile doesn't say much. His mixes though bear the true mark of the Funky Explorator and this is why we are so thrilled to have him join these series. His deep groovy cuts from the 70's prove he is such a fine collector and act as an open invitation to jam around the world. More Strangelove : https://www.mixcloud.com/D_Strangelove/ Dr. Strangelove is funky to the bone the first 20 minutes Gelale pitches the honky tonk scat the next 20 minutes La fabrock keeps surprising the audience the last 20 minutes Enjoy the sounds of La fabrock on Radio 666 and Raptz.
