Back for another outstanding episode of our Funky Explorations ! This time Gelale ( https://www.mixcloud.com/Gelale ) and I proudly welcome The Monk of Fonk aka L.O.O.S. This funky brother from Belgium delivered a wild mix packed with 11 songs so we decided to play along and deliver a wicked 33 tracks set packed with glorious funky gems ! L.O.O.S has been djing and collecting records since he was 17, his mixcloud is full of gorgeous mixes showing a wide scope of incluence, abilities and a generous personality. He's got his own websites and is available for bookings. So wat you waiting for ? Press play and enjoy the mighty sounds of FEX#41 ! More funky snacks : https://www.mixcloud.com/SAMBOO101/ La fabrock sets the night on fire for the first 20 minutes L.O.O.S triggers funky hearts attacks through the next 20 minutes Gelale melts the night away for the last 20 minutes Next episode: Brokers (Belgium) As heard on http://http://radio666.com/ and http://www.raptz.com/
