Best of 2018 (so far) by Les Tontons Transistors (A) | Parliament, Gorillaz, .Paak, James Blake...

It's that time of the year again when we look back at all the amazing music that was release in the first semester of 2018. With a little help from Johnny https://www.mixcloud.com/louiegourmande/ and Alex https://www.mixcloud.com/alexandre-ruiz/, we selected our favourite releases of 2018 and crafted 2 nice little mixes for your greedy ears ! We have about 40 songs for you, 20 this and 20 next week, mainly rap, electro, rnb, funk, rock and a little world music. In this first mix, gorgeous tunes from Kali Uchis, Gorillaz, Grems, Labelle, JPEGMAFIA, Anderson .Paak, Jay Rock, Beyoncé & JAY-Z, Fantastic Negrito, Ignace de Souza & The Melody Aces, Guts, PRhyme, Kadhja Bonet, Parliament, Bishop Nehru, Nikitch & Kuna Maze, Jack White , Busdriver, Dabrye & Jon Wayne, James Blake, Sofiane Saidi, Rejjie Snow, Majestics. Did we forget any ? Tell us in comments ! https://www.facebook.com/tontontransistor/ Radio666.com (http://http://radio666.com/) and Raptz (http://www.raptz.com/)
