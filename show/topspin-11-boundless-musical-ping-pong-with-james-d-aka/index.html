James is probably the André Agassi of Musical Ping-Pong ! No matter, what you send, he has the perfect and cleanest return, leaving you enthusiastic and inspired, knowing that you can pretty much go any direction and he'll love it. In this mix, we tackle Jazz, Rap, Indie Electro, Pop, Rock, Garage and Surf Rock. With artists from Belgium, France, UK, USA and Finland.
