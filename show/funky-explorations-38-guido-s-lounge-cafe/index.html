Gelale ( https://www.mixcloud.com/Gelale ) and I are proud to welcome Guido to this new episode of our funky explorations. From Geleen, Netherlands, he is the master of lounge and ambient on Mixcloud, and humble runner of a multi-awarded show. Guido has been a long time supporter of the show and it's about time we hand him the keys to our Funky Explorations. So get ready for Guido's take on funky music, a thin line between lounge, soul and funk, a.k.a the ideal soundtrack to your coffee break on a sunny café terrace ! Treat yourself : https://www.mixcloud.com/Jazzmo/ Guido takes you for a dive for the first 20 minutes La fabrock carries you to the moon through the next 20 minutes Gelale walks you up to the Blue Mountains for the last 20 minutes Next episode: ATTICBTN (UK) As heard on http://http://radio666.com/ and http://www.raptz.com/
