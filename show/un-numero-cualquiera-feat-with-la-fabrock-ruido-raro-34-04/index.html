Good to all of you, Here I leave you the 34th episode of the series Ruido Raro;)" This episode is special because I have the great pleasure of having the Collab of Mr. https://www.mixcloud.com/lafabrock/ -> a reference of the underground music in its broadest concept (if you do not know him yet, you are losing precious time in following him). Small description of the surprise you prepared -> "A collection of strange sounds, bright or gloomy, seasoned with a few unrelated hip-hop beats, sounds of extinct languages, synthetic voices, analog or digital, movie or video games soundtracks from my favourite experimentators of all time. I then spend some time reflecting on "what is strangeness?" and "what are strange sounds?" in order to find more material. This is how I came up with a few video games soundtracks and the extinct languages skits." Mix composition: (2 parts) 1st 45mins. LaFabRock and 45.45 to end by me Enjoyt. Tracklist in 1st message.
