La fabrock is pleased to present a soulful jazz mix guaranteed 100% covid-free. It features mostly 2019 releases : - the return of Osunlade on Yoruba Records, - UK very creative trumpetist Yazz Ahmed self relased 6th album, - kings of new jazz Ezra Collective, - contemporary Jazz from Japan with Ronin Arkestra, - rap with a reissue of Things Fall Apart the classic roots LP who turned 20 last year. - a live show of Soul brother number 1 recorded in 1969 and released last november. Enjoy. As heard on Radio 666 and Raptz.
