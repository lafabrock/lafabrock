For the 58th episode of our Funky Explorations, Gelale ( mixcloud.com/Gelale ) and I are thrilled to welcome iCazo! From the US and from the world, iCazo owes his love of music to his parents who knew how to make the house sound like a party every weekend. Very early on, discos across America fueled his passion for music and people, and soon he naturally started DJing to share his energy, good vibrations and beauty. When one thinks about iCazo, the first word that comes to mind is generosity; he proves again it with this beautiful set he offers us.
