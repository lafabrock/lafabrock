Gelale ( http://mixcloud.com/Gelale ) and I proudly welcome Vince to our Funky Explorations. Vince is really a sure shot when you come to Funky Explorations. He's he's able to go full dark techno but also deliver all those crazy funkyest edits from the soundcloud nu-funk scene. And the set he put out for us is just mind blowing ! Vince comes from Mulhouse France, he is not a DJ but he runs a real popular mixcloud channel and is open to all kinds of terrific collabs. Check him out on mixcloud and facebook for more about him ! More soul expansion music : https://www.mixcloud.com/DjVince La fabrock stand on top for the first 20 minutes Vince does some true school bombin for the next 20 minutes Gelale crushes some palm trees for the last 20 minutes Next episode: Mauro Vecchi (Italy) As heard on radio666.com and raptz.com
