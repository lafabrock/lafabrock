From the funk infused house of Moodyman, Late Night Tuff Guy, Theo Parrish or Bob Sinclar, to the experimentations of clipping., Clark or Afriqa, this show delivers my favourite new releases (Daedelus, Letherette) as well as a few classics I can't get enough of (Cassius, that Cerrone track !). Don't miss the new Four Tet, Floating Points or Max Cooper. Favourite new album comes french Label Tigersushi, who's releasing an Ambient compilation for which they recruited la fine fleur of french electro, among whom artists who had never done ambient before (Jackson and His computer Band). A lire : l'interview de Joakim pour FIP : https://www.fip.fr/electro/joakim-l-ambient-est-une-musique-qui-se-ressent-physiquement-16943 As heard on Radio 666 and Raptz.
