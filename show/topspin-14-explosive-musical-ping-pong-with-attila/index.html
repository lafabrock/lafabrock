Topspin #14 - Explosive Musical Ping Pong with Attila

En ligne le 25 octobre 201960 min17 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗Écouter sur hearthis ↗
Attila is the man. His wide music knowledge and enthusiasm for music makes him an ideal partner for a musical ping-pong. His raw selections of material for his mixcloud chanel always lead to a satisfying experience and many musical discoveries no matter what you area of expertise is. For this episode we took the time to craft something original and sharp, digging out old classics and showcasing new material. In this game we tackle bluesy rap, hippiyish world music, classy electronic music, nu funk, prog rock and experimental beatmakers from lo-fi to trip-hop. More from Attila : https://www.mixcloud.com/attilaela/ More Topspin : https://www.mixcloud.com/lafabrock/playlists/topspin-musical-ping-pong-with-friends/ Une production by les tontons : facebook.com/tontontransistor Music by : Soul Coughing, G-Love and Special Sauce, Ananda Shankar, Silk Road Assassins, Pink Floyd, The Heavy, Caribou, Hot Chip, Auntie Flo, Howie B and Tricky.
