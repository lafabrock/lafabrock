One thing for sure, this eighth's season of the show won't let you down when it comes to new music discoveries. We blend in old classics with the most exciting new releases, remixes, mesmerizing collaborations, and we polish the transitions to wrap you in the sweetest listening experience. In this episode : *Flume meets Toro Y Moi and get the John Hopkins remix treatment *Avant Garde talent Julianna Barwick meets Nosaj Thing *The return of some of my fav producers Bibio, JLin, Mura Masa, Peaking Lights and The Soft Pink Truth. *Aphex Twin live from the vault of John Peel Sessions *Chilled beats from Denmark with Jonas Munk *Live recording from Leif's exquisite soundtrack of Andy Wharhol 16mm film "Screen Tests" as commissioned by Barbican Centre in Early February *Roger and Brian Eno bring their shade of blue *and Frank Kimbrough brings Thelonious Monk back to life All this and more in episode 2 of La fabrock season 8, as brought to you by Radio 666 and Raptz.
