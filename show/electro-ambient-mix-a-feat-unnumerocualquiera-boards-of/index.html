Electro Ambient Mix A | feat. UnNumeroCualquiera | Boards Of Canada, Brian Eno, I-F, Kraftwerk, Bola

En ligne le 18 février 201975 min33 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗Écouter sur hearthis ↗
A while ago, UnNumeroCualquiera and I collaborated over a Ruido Raros episode (https://www.mixcloud.com/unnumerocualquiera/un-numero-cualquiera-feat-with-la-fabrock-ruido-raro-34-040518/). It was a bliss. UnNumeroCualquiera then sent some electro ambient mix with 90's electronic music vibe : Dj SPooky, Bola, Boards Of Canada, I-F, etc. I had to research those bands and dig back in my own collection. I found similar artists (Luke Vibert, FunkStorüng, Aphex Twin, The Orb), artists that might have inspired (Kraftwerk, Eno, Jarre) or shaped that 90's sound as well as their legacy (113, Gessafelstein, Burial) up to the latest releases (Aesop Rock and Tobacco, Lotic, Richard Devine). The result is a 1h15 min electro ambient nostalgia mix that verges on ambient, IDM, abstract, experimental and hip-hop with tracks from 1977 to 2019. Be sure to pay a visit to the man : https://www.mixcloud.com/unnumerocualquiera/ As heard on http://http://radio666.com/ and http://www.raptz.com/
