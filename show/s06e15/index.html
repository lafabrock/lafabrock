Starting off with Lang Lang playing around with "Chopsticks" (remember Marilyn Monroe in 1955's Billy Wilder's classic Seven Year Itch ?) * this mix escalates quickly to higher level of virtuosity with * new releases from Vansessa Wagner taking on Philip Glass, * Olga Scheps bringing thunder out of her pianoforte * and Yu-Chien Tseng, the 10 M$ violin player, playing the most technical Wieniawski piece on a 7 M$ Guarneri Del Gesù violin. * Before the end of the show, more Lang Lang, more Wanessa Wagner, * Jazz piano with Sun Ra, * OST from Max Richeter and Johann Johannsson and Ken Thorne, * Bach with a piece for organ by Olivier Latry, and the Goldberg Variations by legendary Glenn Gould, * sweetness with Guillaume Coppola taking on a Debussy favourite, * more violin virtuosity with South Korean young talent Sueye Park * ending with some Sitar by Bill Laswell. Hoping to soothe your hearts and ears, lafabrock wishing you a fluffy early June. RAPTZ / RADIO 666
