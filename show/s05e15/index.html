This show : starts in Jaipur India, revolves around the Heliocentrics, caresses you with the silk of a Bach Cantata and the velvet voice of Esther Phillips. then second gears into Big K.R.I.T's gospel-bluesy rap, electrifies you with Go Yama and GTA. a little detour to Sergio Mendes's Brasil then back to India with a famous break by Kashmere Stage band (Holy Calamity !!). then Rap, Funk with Bootsy inviting Musiq Soulchild & Big Daddy Kane to his 2017 album and an Internet celebrated bass cover of a classic Eric Clapton tune by an unknown Polish chick. let's have a few remixes (NxWorries and Alicia Keys remixed) and hit the road again. Destination ? the past, with a French rap classic DJ Vadim and Nightmars On Wax back with new material Anderson .Paak groovin on SiR, Rick Ross with Miguel As seen on radio 666 radio666.com (http://http://radio666.com/) and Raptz (http://www.raptz.com/)
