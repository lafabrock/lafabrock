Gelale ( http://mixcloud.com/Gelale ) and I happily welcome again the one and only Jonathan Field. Our friend from UK began DJing in the early eighties and since then he has never stopped providing our world with the best sounds. Playing several musical genres, the permanent feature in everything he does is that soulful side which we have described as a distinctive funky soulful elegance. Please put it to the test right here: https://www.mixcloud.com/Jonathan_Field/ La fabrock makes the good guys win even in the real life during the first 20 minutes Jonathan Field makes way for the original the next 20 minutes Gelale shakes free through the last 20 minutes As heard on Radio 666 and Raptz.
