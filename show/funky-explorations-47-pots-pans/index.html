Gelale ( http://mixcloud.com/Gelale ) and I proudly welcome Gareth AKA G-Spot, from Toronto Canada. Gareth is the host of the Pots & Pans Radio show and a damn fine DJ. For his selection expect essential Funky beats for hip hop heads, finely mixed with the best DJ skills - I'm talking scratching, beat juggling, the whole deal. We do our best to keep up with the pace and provide our simmered funky explorations. Pots & Pans radio show is not your average radio show, enjoy all his carefully crafted mixes on mixcloud, book him, invite him to your online radio station, somebody please make him famous ! Chune in: https://www.mixcloud.com/PotsinPans La fabrock heats up the saucepan for the first 20 minutes Pots & Pans brings it to a hard boil for the next 20 minutes Gelale nicely deglazes it for the last 20 minutes Next episode: Master Zelig is back !
