Je ne sais pas si 2018 était une bonne ou une mauvaise année mais mixer ensemble cette belle sélection des Tontons était un pur enchantement ! Deuxième épisode ! Maintenant ! ____________________ Back with a second episode of our super eclectic year end mix. We selected 60 songs released in the second half of 2018 and crafted 3x 1 hour mixes with them. In this second mix, you'll get music by Anthony Joseph, Chaka Khan, Mac Miller, JazzaNova, Gangue, Nas, Oh Sees and more. Come back next week for final episode. Follow us : https://www.facebook.com/tontontransistor/ More tontons : https://www.mixcloud.com/lafabrock/playlists/les-tontons-transistors-best-of/ Les Tontons Transistors are : fab, from La fabrock Johnny from https://www.mixcloud.com/louiegourmande/ Alex from https://www.mixcloud.com/alexandre-ruiz/ Radio666.com (http://http://radio666.com/) and Raptz (http://www.raptz.com/) Visuals by https://laurentlabat.com/
