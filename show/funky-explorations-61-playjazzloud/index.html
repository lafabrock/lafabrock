We're so blessed to have another contribution by Julian from PlayJazzLoud ! Gelale ( https://www.mixcloud.com/Gelale/ ) and I are huge fans of his channel and we're thrilled to bring you his 2nd contribution to the Funky Explorations series ! Julian is from LA and he spins great jazz mixes and much more on his Mixcloud channel. Brace yourself, Julian has some obscure classics and gorgeous breaks in store for you and of course you know we love to play along ! Play Jazz louder : https://www.mixcloud.com/playjazzloud/ 20 minutes each : PlayJazzLoud opens with Time and space La fabrock puts on his best maquillage for the middle section Gelale breaks all taboos for the end Proud to be hosted on Radio 666 and Raptz.
