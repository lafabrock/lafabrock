Say kids what episode is it ? It's episode 50 and this means you're in for a treat. For this very special episode Gelale ( https://www.mixcloud.com/Gelale/ ) and I are pleased to re-welcome an early contributor and long time supporter of the show: NiggaG0d. From the lovely island of Barbados, bravely facing the waves of the mighty Atlantic ocean, he delivers a funkiest show full all time classics of neat breaks. Gelale and I play along and dust off some cool grooves. It's a party and you're invited ! Thanks to all the contributors of these wonderful series. Long live the Funky Explorations. More Godness: https://www.mixcloud.com/niggagod/ La fabrock sends a message for the first 20 minutes NiggaG0d goes deep for the next 20 minutes Gelale keeps on gassin for the last 20 minutes Next : Jonathan Field (UK) As heard on radio666.com and raptz.com
