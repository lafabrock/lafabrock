Funky Corners Show #218 Featuring La Fabrock 05-07-2016

Diffusée le 7 mai 2016en ligne le 13 mai 2016120 min41 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗
Happy Mother’s Day! Show #218 is a bouquet of funk and hip hop for your mum, and about yo’ momma. La Fabrock joins the program with a 35 minute set straight from the 666 of Northwest France. It’s about the patterns. Jurassic 5 creatively flips Clive Hicks’ track “Look Hear,” in the Crate Highlight segment of the show. Selections include: 2Pac, Audio Two, Big Ben Beats, Blood Orange, Hamilton Bohannon, Les DeMerle, Missy Elliott, Ghostface Killah, Goodie Mob, Gravediggaz, Herbie Hancock, The Herbaliser, Thirstin Howl III, K-Solo, King Tee, Miike Snow, The Moments, Nas, The Pharcyde, Private Investigators, Psychoegyptian, Pudgee Tha Phat Bastard, Queen Latifah, Buddy Rich, Johnny “Guitar” Watson, Yomo And Maulkie, Youssoupha, and M.O.M.
