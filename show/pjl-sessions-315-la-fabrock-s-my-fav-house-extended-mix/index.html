PJL Sessions #315 [La Fabrock's My Fav House Extended Mix]

En ligne le 4 mars 202490 min45 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗
The only thing I like better than music is people. So an invitation to exchange tracks over messenger to build a pool of song for a mix is a nobrainer to me. Even more so when it comes from @playjazzloud ! It was supposed to be just one mix by Julian but the selection grew on me and I felt like mixing it. Before I knew it, I was digging deep in my vaults, adding personal favorites, as well as classic house anthems, and spending way too much time crafting the mix. The result is this 46 songs in 90 minutes mix covering 4 decades of house music (1992-2024). Whether you like acid, jazz, latin, hip-hop, french filtered, disco, synthetic, pop, bass, or even live-band. We provide originals, rares, remixes, overheard classics, hidden gems, famous samples you name it. All the love goes out to the artist and the dancers. And to Julian for reigniting the house fire in me ! Welcome to our house ! Original mix : https://www.mixcloud.com/playjazzloud/pjl-sessions-198-at-the-jazz-club
