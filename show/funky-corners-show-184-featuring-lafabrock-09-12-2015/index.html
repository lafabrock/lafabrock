Funky Corners Show #184 Featuring LaFabrock 09-12-2015

Diffusée le 12 septembre 2015en ligne le 25 janvier 2016120 min42 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗
The first quarter of show #184 features mostly instrumentals, including chopped beats and temperamental soundtrack-jazz-type-stuff. LaFabrock later infiltrates the proceedings and gives us a bass-heavy rap and beats set. We top it all off with an extended Crate Highlight. Roy Ayers brings US “Sensitize,” and we bring YOU 10 tracks that sampled it. Selections include: 7L & Esoteric, Ab-Soul, Big K.R.I.T., Black Rob, Bornze Nazareth, Dennis Brown, Cap1, Crime Fam, Disiz, DJ Racy A.J., Grand Agent, Guts, Jeru The Damaja, Kisszanto, MC Eiht, Franco Micalizzi, Hudson Mohawke, O.C., Jay Rock, Rustie, Eric Ryan, Slim Thug, SomehowArt, Statik Selektah, Tobacco, Twisted Angels, Les Vikings, and Junglep***y.
