Funky Corners Show #178 Featuring LaFabrock 08-01-2015

Diffusée le 1 août 2015en ligne le 4 janvier 2016121 min35 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗
Straight from France, LaFabrock steps up and delivers an abbreviated, but commanding guest set on Funky Corners 178. We also bring the soul back, and contribute an assortment of various rap flavors for any discerning palette. An appetizing plate of funk is served up for the Crate Highlight, in the form of “Rule Of Mind,” by 9th Creation. Selections include: 3rd Bass, Black Knights, Black Moon, Blackbyrds, Brick, James Brown, Danger Doom, DJ Jazzy Jeff, Fashawn, Ronnie Foster, Gang Starr, King Tee, Kool & The Gang, Lord Finesse, Main Source, Idris Muhammed, OutKast, Red Black & Green, Run-D.M.C., Erick Sermon, Slick Rick, Socialistik, Special Ed, Tosca, Billy Wright, and Del The Funky Homosapien.
