This third episode of our Funky Explorations is made with fire and fury, love and exuberance, friendship and expertise, for a short hour of pure Funk passion! As usual, the most eclectic man alive, https://www.mixcloud.com/lafabrock/, is by my side for our explorations, and he keeps rocking the house! Today, we are very honored and happy to welcome T. Wheels, the encyclopedia of Hip-Hop sampling, the passionate Funk digger, the fervent Soul seeker, the ruler of the B-Boys and the sovereign of the Beat junkies. He is well known as https://www.mixcloud.com/FunkyCorners/. This time, we come to offer you a show full of funky gems with some rarities from all over the globe. Funky Corners delights you during the first 20 minutes La fabrock keeps the temperature rising until minutes 40 Gelale tries to maintain your feet hot for the last 20 minutes artwork by laurentlabat.com
