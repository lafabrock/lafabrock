This used to be a radio show. Now it's more like an introspection, a meditation, a therapy, a stream of consciousness where songs from my heart collide into a fizzy goey never-know-what's-the-next-track thingy. I won't lie. I love it. I am aware the transitions are not DJ school approved but I'm waaaaaay beyond that. Doin' it for the fun. There are plenty more serious djs out there. Songs in here range from 1963 to 2023 with a heavy focus on the 2006>2011 span. Go to comments to see those songs ranked by released date. Notes : - Ottawan was a band produced by Thomas Bangalter's (half daf punk) father - I saw a video of Manu Chao playing that remix of his song on a DJ set. Wasn't ready. - That final skit in the Skyy song is so cute - Super Sayians for Liam and Elih, Love is all for Ethan's Bday. - Disco Inferno was the song that would play in Dungeon Keeper 2 when a creature won jackpot at the casino.
