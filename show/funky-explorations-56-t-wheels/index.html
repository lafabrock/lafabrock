Today, La fabrock ( mixcloud.com/lafabrock ) and I are so glad to welcome T_Wheels for his second contribution to our Funky Explorations! We do love when our Funk Citizens are pleased to pay the Funk tax. Coming from Albany, NY, USA, T_Wheels is well known for his weekly Funky Corners show broadcasted on several radio stations. Once, we introduced him as the encyclopedia of Hip-Hop sampling, the passionate Funk digger, the ruler of the B-Boys and the sovereign of the Beat junkies, it still works!
