Human [with La Fabrock]

En ligne le 19 avril 201864 min36 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗
This week I'm extemely pleased to play host to La Fabrocks initiatory Human session. In his own words "I've put a lot of time and effort in making it as Human as I could". As always with Mr La Fabrock, there's a wide array of music from all spheres jammed together in a rather appealing fashion. A perfect mix to accompany the improvement in weather. As always there's a link below - if you're not accustomed with his shiz get over to his page now and support the hell out of the artists. Next week another debut from the wonderful Eclectic Ladyland Live Show - Stay toooned. Love to you all, you all x https://www.mixcloud.com/lafabrock/
