Topspin #04 - Funky Musical Ping-Pong with Scottieboyuk

En ligne le 1 février 201847 min18 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗Écouter sur hearthis ↗
Here is a new episode in our TopSpin series where we invite friends to play musical Ping-Pong over messenger to collect materials for a mix. This week, I am most pleased to welcome one of the early enthusiast of this concept and a fine player, expert digger, vynil addict with access to a vast collection of records, NessRadio DJ, the kind of player you won't catch off guard: Scottie Boy ! In this mix you will find Scottie's love for dub and reggae music, disco, deep grooves, incredible remixes and hard to find Funk and Soul gems. But above all Scottie's generosity who extended his original contribution with songs that I had to include in the mix.
