Gelale ( http://mixcloud.com/Gelale ) and I proudly welcome Uroš Jevtić AKA The Captain Who Went To Lunch as our next guest and first contributor from Belgrade, Serbia. As you'll soon realize, this captain loves vinyls, bananas, real underground hip hop, but has a low tolerance to sugar and weak beats. For us put up a very hip hop focused funky exploration with heavy hitters in the likes of Big L, Lord Finesse or Big Daddy Kane. The Captain Who Went To Lunch serves a weekly two hours sugar free banana split radio show on mixcloud. Check him out for fat beats and heart felt presentations of the music he loves. More splits: https://www.mixcloud.com/the_captain_who_went_to_lunch/ The Captain splits bananas for the first 20 minutes Gelale wants to be your CB for the next 20 minutes La fabrock makes it blend for the last 20 minutes Next episode: Pots And Pans Radio (Canada) As heard on radio666.com and raptz.com
