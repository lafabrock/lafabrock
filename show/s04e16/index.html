S04e16 | Tell Me Sutra | 2nd of February 2016

Diffusée le 2 février 2016en ligne le 2 février 201764 min15 morceaux

This is the last episode in the funk-soul-jazz-world section, next week we'll be back with an all electronic show. So enjoy this one ! It's full of the gems I collected over the last few months : James Brown, Gregory Isaacs for the big names, a lot of jazz standards revisited (Mona Lisa, Summertime, Nuages), and all the smoothness that can bring some piano, saxophone or double bass virtuoso solos. Smooth but not cold as we warm things up with some carribean reggae niceness, wild afrofunk, psychedelic samba, and a famous massive attack sample hidden somewhere. Compilation of the month : Doing It in Lagos: Boogie, Pop & Disco in 1980s Nigeria https://www.soundwayrecords.com/Shop/DownloadDetails?rid=SWR_RE_116 The show finishes with a reinterpretation of Philip Glass by Bruce Levingston featuring a sutra/poem reading by Ethan Hawke. Meditate, drink beer, shit in your neighbour's garden, and do the rock. Respectfully.
