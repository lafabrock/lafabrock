Back at you with a sweet little mix of my favourite new tracks in World, Jazz, Dancehall and House. Expect : *A crazy polka-emo remix of Rihanna *Dancehall session with new Gyptian *Lots of Brasilian sweetness *A short trip to Saint-Tropez with the original sample for Dimitri From Paris classic tune "Sacré Français" *An hommage to Nelson Mandela *Some cool party tunes to shake your booty with new Cassius and a Of Montreal *Wyclef Jean back to house on the new Gorgon City *Blues and traditional Syrian music to finish on a cool note. Work hard, clean thoroughly and do the rock respectfully.
