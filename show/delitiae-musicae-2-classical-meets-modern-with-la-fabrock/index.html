Delitiae Musicae 2 (Classical meets Modern) with La Fabrock

En ligne le 7 octobre 201860 min19 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗
All good things come in threes : ) Given that we relish melting classical and contemporary, and many friends absolutely enjoyed our first collab, LaFab and I enthusiastically worked on this 2nd chapter. Dakar Suite by Leni Stern, album which exerts a strong attraction on me, was inspirational. LaFab says: “In this mix, I do my best to walk in this thin line of ecstatic and uplifting music that Magda set for us, while incorporating my main obsessions: late releases, unexpected piano covers, vibrating modern cello pieces and a touch of exoticism. Blissfully yours, La fabrock”. P.S.: at minute 32:00 I always feel shivers down my spine.
