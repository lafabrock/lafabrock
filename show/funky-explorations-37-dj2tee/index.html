Gelale ( https://www.mixcloud.com/Gelale ) and I are proud to welcome DJ2TEE to this new episode of our funky explorations. From Leeds, UK comes a man of many talents in including DJ, novelist, musician and designer. His main interest lies in Soul and Jazz but rest asure that he fits perfectly within these Funky Explorations series. His choice of themes speak for themselves : literate and wise, demanding and loyal to the craft with an amazing ability to synthetize a vast musical knowledge in a coherent mix. I bet you can't wait to see what he put together for you in this epsiode ! Do yourself a favour : https://www.mixcloud.com/tony-todd/. Gelale conjures up beats bells and sounds for the first 20 minutes DJ2TEE explores the eight corners of funk through the next 20 minutes La fabrock digs deeper until the well's gone dry Next episode: Guido (NL) As heard on http://http://radio666.com/ and http://www.raptz.com/
