Funky Explorations #43 (Dj Snip)

En ligne le 2 juillet 201960 min14 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗Écouter sur hearthis ↗
Gelale ( https://www.mixcloud.com/Gelale ) and I proudly welcome yet another Belgian DJ to these series : Dj Snip. Coming from Charleroi, his heart dwells in Chicago and Detroit. He loves his SL1210 turntables, funk, house music but above all, he loves to make us dance. Snip runs a radio show on Radio Charleking, the Ergo Dj School and is a member of the Dj Crew Basual People. For us he put together some sweet and freaky 70's and 00's funky tunes even including one of his production where he cleverly flips a classic Erykah Badu track. We tag along and offer new and classic funky tunes for your delight, wishing you a hot and groovy summer. More from our guest : https://www.mixcloud.com/djsnip Snip gets cosmic for the first 20 minutes Gelale gets ghetto drunk for the next 20 minutes La fabrock makes the temperature rise for the last 20 minutes Next episode: Vince (France) As heard on radio666.com and raptz.com
