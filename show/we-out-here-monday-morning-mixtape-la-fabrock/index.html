We Out Here Monday Morning Mixtape: La fabrock

En ligne le 18 mai 201960 min16 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗
Here is something I don't usually do : contests. I love Stamp The Wax and especially Monday Mixtapes. They kind of mean a lot to me. And since so many of my good mixcloud friends played the game Liza, Roosticman, PlayJazzLoud, Jazzcat, Zelyg, L.O.O.S, I thought I would play along. Now this is long overdue but nevermind, it was fun, trying to match the MM format. Enjoy chilled and warm sounds from France to Hawaii, from Brasil to India, with this subtle lazy monday morning "i dont wanna go back to work" vibe and field recordings from the streets of mexico since STW seems fond of tourism too. Some very new releases (Vampire Weekend, Seba Kaapstad, Comet Is Coming, Paper Tiger, Holly Henderson), re-releases (Moodog) as well as stuff i've had stuck in my head for years (G. Love, Ratatat, Flying Lotus, Jeanne Moreau). The contest : https://www.stampthewax.com/2019/04/15/we-out-here-festival-monday-morning-mixtape-competition/ As Aired on radio 666 and on Raptz
