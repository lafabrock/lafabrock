Elle est pas belle la vie ? ------------- [20 good reasons to go to Beauregard] This is me having fun mashing my favourite tunes from the artists in the lineup of Beauregard, a huge festival in my city. For some artists I picked their most recent releases, for others I picked classic hits from their discography. There will be another mix like this next week. If ever, you come to this festival let me know, my house is 2 km from the premises and I have a pass for Saturday.
