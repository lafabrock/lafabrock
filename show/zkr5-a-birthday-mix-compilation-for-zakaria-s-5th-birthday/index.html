ZKR5 | A Birthday Mix Compilation for Zakaria's 5th Birthday

En ligne le 16 mai 201960 min23 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗Écouter sur hearthis ↗
My son was recently invited to his friend Zakaria's birthday party. So the whole family made a good old fashioned CD compilation with tunes we loved and others that sounded appropriate for when you turn 5. Thought you guys might like it as well. We did our own cover by colouring stripes with pencils and added a big black Z on top. The inside of the cover had some nice words, silly jokes and a very approximative tracklisting. Loads of fun.
