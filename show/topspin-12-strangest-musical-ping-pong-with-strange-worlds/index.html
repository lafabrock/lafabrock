Topspin #12 | Strangest Musical Ping-Pong with Strange Worlds

En ligne le 7 mai 201986 min17 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗Écouter sur hearthis ↗
Strange World and I go way back in terms of mixcloud friendship. He's been a long time supporter of my show and it's impossible not to admire his vast musical knowledge and ability to paint strange and sometimes spooky, sometimes freaky musical landscapes with a wide array of musical genres and mixing techniques. When I started the topspin series I was looking forward to get to ping pong with my man with multiple aliases, knowing we would never fit our wide musical obsessions in one hour. So here is our wild mix of tiny but essential part of our musical knowledge with Mr Bungle, Kraftwerk, Jean michel Jarre, Señor Coconut, Brain Damage, LFO, Underworld, Bicep, FSOL and Rervolting Cocks.
