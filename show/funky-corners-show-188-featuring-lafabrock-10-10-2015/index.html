Funky Corners Show #188 Featuring LaFabrock 10-10-2015

Diffusée le 10 octobre 2015en ligne le 25 janvier 2016120 min45 morceaux

LaFabrock steps back in the spot with the first part of a 2-show guest mix. Also featured on the show is another round of local music and some upbeat jazz-funk. Find out who sampled “Harlem River Drive,” and how they flipped it, in the Crate Highlight portion of the show. Selections include: 6ix, Alpine, Ammoncontact, Anitek, Bohannon, Shyste Chronkyte, Finsta Bundy, Ben Golson, Group Home, Kool G. Rap, John Klemmer, Billy Larkin, Mic Lanny, Jay Rock, Scratch Bandits Crew, Tweet, UZ, and Ben Grim.
