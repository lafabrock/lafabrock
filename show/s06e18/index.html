Electro freaks rejoice ! La fabrock is here for you ! The comet is coming is a unclassifiable monster that offers the best of electro, punk and jazz. That Larry Levan remix of La Compagnie Creole is epic. The Unfolding is the original sample of a Chemical Brothers classic. Mr Oizo is a personal god. Silk Road Assassins deliver. The Chemical Brothers too. Meitei and Biosphere bring some smoothness to the mixture. All 2019 releases except for that good old Propellerheads. Enjoy the sounds of La fabrock on Radio 666 and Raptz.
