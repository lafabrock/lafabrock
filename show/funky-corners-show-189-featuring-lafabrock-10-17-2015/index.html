Funky Corners Show #189 Featuring LaFabrock 10-17-2015

Diffusée le 17 octobre 2015en ligne le 26 janvier 2016120 min48 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans l'archiveÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗
As advertised, our buddy from France, LaFabrock is back with Part 2 of his guest set. He takes us into funk, jazz, rap, bass and even a dab of 80s pop. We whip up some old school music to start up, and finish off the show. T_Wheels reveals his favorite Diamond D song and plays the original sample, in the Crate Highlight. Selections include: Marcel Amont, Bonde Do Role, Bugs In The Attic, Busta Rhymes, Chaundon, Digital Underground, EPMD, Eric B. & Rakim, Benny Golson, Jungle Brothers, PJ Katz, Michael Zerang & The Blue Lights, Naughty By Nature, Nice & Smooth, Public Enemy, Patrice Rushen, Travis Scott, Sly & The Family Stone, Dionne Warwick, and Das EFX.
