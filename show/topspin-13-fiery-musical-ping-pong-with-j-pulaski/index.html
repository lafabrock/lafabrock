Topspin #13 - Fiery Musical Ping Pong with J.Pulaski / Innamissions

En ligne le 23 mai 201960 min18 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗Écouter sur hearthis ↗
I love Jason. His weekly 2 hour show innamissions (innamissions.com) is the perfect source for the best new music and a continual source of inspiration for my shows : simple, effective, documented and clean. Inviting him for a musical ping pong proved to be the best thing I could do. Our tos and fros were rich, friendly and detailed, basically the main reason why anyone would want to share music with someone. World, Funk, Electro, Rap and Beats. Artists from Europe to Japan, to the US. Old gems, latest releases and personal favorites. More from Jason : http://www.mixcloud.com/innamissions More Topspin : https://www.mixcloud.com/lafabrock/playlists/topspin-musical-ping-pong-with-friends/ Une production by les tontons : http://www.facebook.com/tontontransistor Music by : Mynio Crusader, Ibibio Sound Machine, Clap Clap, Gangue, Sun Ra, Notorious B.I.G, Rejoicer, Paper Tiger and Amon Tobin.
