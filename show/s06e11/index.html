For this episode, you'll get new music from all over the musical spectrum (rock, rap, electro) with new releases from late 2018 and early 2019. First single for an upcoming UNKLE album, Legendary producer Gal McDermot gets the Souleance treatment (fee&legal compatible) James Blake invites Outkast's André 3000 French multi award winning OST crafter Alexandre Desplat RJD2 in rare form in late 2018 Mind bending belgium rock electro band De Staat is back with a gorgeous video and a huge single Tenacious D is back with a new project (hear Jack Black rap over silly trap beat) French rock superband TIGRAMOTO is ready to take over the world Quality beats by Statik Selektah, Funky DL, Elaquent and Dj Muggs Quality lyrics by Earl Sweatshirt, MOP, Black Thought Four Tet, Photay, Apparat, Mura Masa and Xiu Xiu and John Zorn bring an organic experimental electronic touch Enjoy and do the rock !
