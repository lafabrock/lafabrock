In this brand new season of lafabrock we're clearly not bothering with : - new releases, we play our favourite tunes from the last 10 decades - genres, we play rap, house, EDM, pop, techno, rock, punk, swing, tango or mambo - taste, we play the good, the bad and the ugly regardless of the current hype And all this for a simple reason : it's about fun and the energy that the music brings. We do bother with mixing all those songs as tight as possible because great things happen when songs collide. Take care, get together cos it's all that matters.
