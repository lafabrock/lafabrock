Topspin #15 - Deceptive Musical Ping Pong with Funkologie

En ligne le 13 octobre 202160 min15 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗Écouter sur hearthis ↗
You'd think that by putting 2 huge funk heads around a ping pong 🏓 table to slice up and throw their deep heart-felt tunes you would end up with the ultimate funky mix 🏆. Think again. DJs are like onions 🧅. It's all about peel layers. Today's guest comes from Switzerland and he is mostly famous for building a trustworthy community 👬 of funky music lovers built from scratch from sharing exquisite mixes and quality 🥇 blog posts about the music he loves most. I urge you to check out https://funkologie.com/ It's the one great thing about these Topspin series : they are a great opportunity to explore my favourite djs musical background. It's like a confessional session and it's made of early musical discoveries, teenage music love affairs, memorable live performance and it all comes from the ❤ More from Funkologie : https://www.mixcloud.com/porkhead/ More Topspin : https://www.mixcloud.com/lafabrock/playlists/topspin-musical-ping-pong-with-friends/
