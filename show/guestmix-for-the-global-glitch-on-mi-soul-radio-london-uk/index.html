A 40 minutes handcrafted for the Global Glitch radio show on Mi Soul Radio. Some classics that defined this global glitchy sound and some recent bangers that I’ve been playing in my show lately. Taking you all around the world, from Rio to Cuba, Paris to Sana’a, London to Kinshasa, Tokyo to Budapest, Lisbon to Kingston. Plus we have a few classic Mario Kart sound effects for the gamer in you ! Get Global ! Get Glitchy ! And Let’s Rock !
