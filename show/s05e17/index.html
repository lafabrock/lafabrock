After this epic contribution to the HUMAN series hosted by Jellylegs ( https://www.mixcloud.com/gazhughes1/human-with-la-fabrock/ ) - a bold attempt to sumup all the music I've ever loved in my life in one hour #oncealife - I am very pleased to offer a show fully dedicated to Jazz music with records spanning from 1956 to 2018 from the likes of Charlie Parker, Rahsaan Roland Kirk, Sun Ra, Ramsey Lewis, Sonny Rollins, The Bad Plus and Sons Of Kemet. This is the first time that I publish a show only about Jazz. I hope you dig. Also, I've found my Michel Gondry intro -Yay!- and a new L-A-F-A-B-R-O-C-K jingle. Keep on rocking !
