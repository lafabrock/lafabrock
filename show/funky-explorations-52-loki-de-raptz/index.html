About 5 years ago, Gelale received a phone call from a Canadian guy whose nickname is Loki. He was launching a WebRadio in Paris and was looking for great music to broadcast... A few weeks later, Fab joined the adventure. A few months later, Fab and Gelale created the Funky Explorations series. Today, for the 52nd episode, they're happy to invite the brain and heart of Radio RapTz, Loki himself! RapTz is a participative media with more than sixty different shows. The programming is built with the desire to offer a wide range of music and promote social and cultural initiatives. On RapTz, all DJs, producers and reporters are passionate volunteers, and there is no advertising in order to guarantee complete freedom of expression. Radio RapTz is simply Radiant, so is Loki! : www.raptz.com Loki shows his funky fantasy during the first 20 minutes Gelale keeps his eyes on the merchandise the next 20 minutes La fabrock brings pills for your ills through the last 20 minutes
