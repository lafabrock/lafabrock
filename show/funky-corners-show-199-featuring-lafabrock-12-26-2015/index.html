Funky Corners Show #199 Featuring LaFabrock 12-26-2015

Diffusée le 26 décembre 2015en ligne le 26 janvier 2016120 min47 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗
LaFabrock is back for the final program of 2015. He brings his usual unusual mix of rap, soul, bass and madness. T_Wheels rounds off the show with new rap and female disco-funk. The Crate Highlight spotlights the B-Boy theme “Put The Music Where Your Mouth Is.” We roll through nine tracks which sampled it. Selections include: Ab-Soul, Bobby Ross Avila, B.B. & The Underground Kingz, Erykah Badu, Blackalicious, Dennis Brown, KRS-One, The Meters, Melba Moore, Mr. Muthaf****n’ eXquire, Bishop Nehru, Jay Prince, Rick Rock, St. Germain, Mavis Staples, Maggie Thrett, and Manny Fresh
