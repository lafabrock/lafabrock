Topspin #07 - Spacy Musical Ping-Pong with Gelale

En ligne le 9 avril 201844 min13 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗Écouter sur hearthis ↗
After these couple of years following Gelale as a fan and collaborator, (https://www.mixcloud.com/lafabrock/playlists/la-fabrock-gelales-funky-explorations/), the only explanation I have for his genius is that he is otherworldly. When his funklecticity collides with my total lack of musical boundaries that means you need to suit up for a stellar journey into sound, jumping from the still earth of Bernard Hermann to the exoplanet of The Weeknd partying with Foo X-Fighters, heading to the Aloé Blacc Star, invading 90's abstract french rap grounding in world beats, and reaching out for hybrids of jazz. <3 https://www.mixcloud.com/Gelale/ __________________________ On part avec Gelale à travers la galaxie à la recherche de la grande question que nous nous posons tous : mais pourquoi est-il si Génial ? Seule explication possible : une origine extra-terrestre, en enfant sauvé du péril d'une civilisation avancée et envoyé sur terre pour répandre l'amour et le funk.
