Funky Explorations #54 (Liza DJ)

En ligne le 17 avril 202063 min21 morceaux

Gelale ( http://mixcloud.com/Gelale ) and I put on our Funky Explorers cap once more to welcome Liza DJ to these series. Now Liza is hard to pin, unless you consider "make you feel good DJ" as a full on category. She's friendly, gifted and her mixes are bound to put a smile on your face. She's rocking the mixcloud world and beyond with her funky mixes, coffee bean series, loose and juicy deliveries. Gelale and I have been fans from day 1 and I can't think of a reason why she hadn't been featured on these series before. For you she explores funk from Africa to Japan with a few stopovers in regrooved-legendary-tracks land. More colorful shades of Liza : https://www.mixcloud.com/Liza_Dj/ Gelale makes starts the chain reaction during the first 20 minutes Liza brings brings the chocolate chip for the next 20 minutes La fabrock invites you to unwind yourelves through the last 20 minutes As heard on Radio 666 and Raptz
