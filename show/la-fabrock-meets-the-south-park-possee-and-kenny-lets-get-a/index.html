La fabrock Meets The South Park Possee and Kenny | Let’s Get A Girl Like You | (Collab)

En ligne le 27 septembre 201578 min56 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗
So what happen is Kenny reached sent 10 incredible songs so they could get the fabrock treatment, best characterized as “mashed into an improbable ragdoll of random tracks”. No bad surprises, no angry metal track bleeding over the mix, no druggy techno beats, no weird experimental tracks. Instead here’s a friendly ride to rediscover 80’s power rock ballads, more rock classics, a few mashups and covers, all fueled by classic movie soundtrack cuts and with the help of a few south park characters we’ll get by.
