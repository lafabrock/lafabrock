The show features a guest mix by DJ Bon Gout from Belgium. DJ Bon Goût delivers a very original mix full of weird synth pop songs and other genre defying goodness, funny audio clips, irrevenrential remixes and covers. His DJ name (DJ Good Taste) shades an interesting 2nd level of reading to what he does that is very much in keeping with the concept of La fabrock ! Enjoy and show some love ! Listen to the extended version https://www.mixcloud.com/DJ_Bon_Gout/ The first part of the show is your usual selection of great new music RAP Prodigy, Papoose & Raekwon Kanye West Travis Scott Missy Elliott Jay-Z, Meek Mill, Rick Ross & French Montana on the new DJ Khaled mixtape Youssoupha POP Miike Snow Blood Orange METAL | The Black Dahlia Murder JAZZ | Buddy Rich REMIXERS John Talabot > Jamie XX Dennis Kruissen > No Doubt Pharrell Williams > A Tribe Called Quest SAMPLED | OC's Time's Up original sample is also a Beatles cover !
