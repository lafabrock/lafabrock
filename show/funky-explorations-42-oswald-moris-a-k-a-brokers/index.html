Funky Explorations #42 (Oswald Moris A.K.A Brokers)

En ligne le 12 juin 201961 min19 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗Écouter sur hearthis ↗
Gelale ( https://www.mixcloud.com/Gelale ) and I proudly welcome Oswal Moris to our Funky Explorations. He's another vinyl lover in these series, he's from Belgium and he's half of the DJ Duo "broke and groovy" Brokers ! Oswald runs a monthly radio show on The Word Radio and once you've listened to what he put together for us you're bound to book him for your next party ! Check him out on mixcloud and facebook for more about him ! In this mix we verge on the rap side of funk with heavy grooves, vicious MC's, old school vibes and brand new releases pushing the borders of the genre! More soul expansion music : https://www.mixcloud.com/brokers/ Gelale gets philthy freaky for the first 20 minutes La fabrock hits you with his Karate Chop for the next 20 minutes Oswald does his groove thang for the last 20 minutes Next episode: DJ Snip (Belgium again!)
