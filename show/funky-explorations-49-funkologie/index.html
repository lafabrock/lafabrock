Funky Explorations #49 (Funkologie)

En ligne le 2 décembre 201960 min17 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗Écouter sur hearthis ↗
Gelale ( http://mixcloud.com/Gelale ) and I proudly Funkologie to our Funky Explorations. Here is another Funk enthusiast, who loves to study and pay tribute to funky music from the root to the fruit : just like us he demands music from the soul and he is ready to embrace any kind of funk as long as it is real. His weapons of choice is a public facebook group as well as a site and mixcloud page. For us he delivers a set that dripples in gorgeous bass sounds and digs as deep as it flies high. Gelale and I jam along. Go study some funk : https://www.mixcloud.com/porkhead Funkologie teaches the funk for the first 20 minutes Gelale amplifies the next 20 minutes La fabrock ends it pas du tout tout doux for the last 20 minutes Next episode: niggaG0d (Barbados) As heard on radio666.com and raptz.com
