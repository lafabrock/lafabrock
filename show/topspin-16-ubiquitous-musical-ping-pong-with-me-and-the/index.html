Topspin #16 - Ubiquitous Musical Ping Pong with Me And The Moroccan

En ligne le 30 mai 202260 min17 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗Les morceaux en playlist YouTube ↗
Fellow mixclouder @meandthemoroccan is releasing his 3rd single 🍾 !! A fine excuse to invite him over for a topspin ! For 2 weeks, we went back and forth🏓 over messenger with some of our favourite tunes in an attempt for me to bridge the gap between his super bouncy 🦘 single "Brozeur", the unearthly retro-futuristic👽 "Alien With Disabilities" and the moody vocorised 🤖 "With Me". Hard to pin the man's down. Somewhere between NY, Paris and Casablanca, somewhere between Electro, Pop, Punk and World music, thrives the sound of Fadil, always on the move 🧳, always unexpected, always vibrant with positive energy. His choices include music from Susobrino, Hiatus Kaiyote, Har Mar Superstar, and The Rapture. Show him some love below, follow him (https://www.instagram.com/me_andthe_moroccan/) and find out more about the untold story of K7 tape gone astray in his latest video (https://youtu.be/5KohqL9UTxU) Track 4 only available at 🍍🌲📀 https://hawaiibonsairecords.bandcamp.com/
