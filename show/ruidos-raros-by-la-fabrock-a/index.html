Ruidos Raros by La fabrock - A

En ligne le 9 mai 201846 min25 morceaux

A collection of strange sounds, bright or gloomy, seasoned with a few unrelated hip-hop beats, sounds of extinct languages, synthetic voices, analog or digital, movie or video games soundtracks from my favourite experimentators of all time. I don't know much about ambient or illbient though I really enjoy listening to records from these genre, so I racked my brain trying to think back of all the artists or songs that had strange sounds. I then spend some time reflecting on "what is strangeness?" and "what are strange sounds?" in order to find more material. This is how I came up with a few video games soundtracks and the extinct languages skits. I tried to come up with a large selection because this is how I like to work : I like to pack my sets with a lot of different songs and artists even if it means not listening to songs in full.
