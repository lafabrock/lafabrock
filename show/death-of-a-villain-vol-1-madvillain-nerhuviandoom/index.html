On the 31st of December 2020 the rap world was shook to learn about the death of one of the most respected underground rapper of the last 2 decades, Daniel Dumile, AKA DOOM, METAL FACE or MF DOOM (All caps when you spell the name). Often described as "your favorite rappers favorite rapper", he leaves behind an impressive discography. Appart from the seminal album Madvillainy, in collaboration with Madlib, he will be remembered for his collaborations with a wide array of producers and rappers including Danger Mouse (DANGERDOOM), Jneiro Jarel (JJ DOOM), Czarface, Bishop Nerhu, Ghostface but also Gorillaz, Radiohead's Thom Yorke, Prince Paul, J Dilla, The Avalanches, Tuxedo or BADBADNOTGOOD. A series of 3 mixes compiling my favourite joints and celebrating the power of his rhyming, mad producing skills, and the genius of his musical choices.
