Topspin is a new series in which I invite guests to play musical ping-pong via messenger to collect 11 tracks use them to create a mix. This week's guest is Pierre Raingeard, a man who devoted his musical life to collecting and curating music available for free on the Internet. Say hello to Pierre : http://mixcloud.com/pierre-raingeard http://ptitbazar-free-legal.blogspot.fr http://facebook.com/ptitbazargratuitetlegal All tracks available here http://soundcloud.com/lafabrock/sets/topspin02 ------------------- Dans la fabrock on est fous. Assez fous pour aller défier un champion sur son propre terrain. Ce champion, c'est Pierre "Free&Legal" Raingeard, grand missionnaire de la musique libre et gratuite, le genre de type qui remplit des playlists soundcloud en trempant des tartines, et le bienveillant parrain des Tontons Transistors.
