Topspin #09 - Dark Side of The Funky Corners (Musical Ping-Pong)

En ligne le 14 juin 201847 min11 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans l'archiveÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗Écouter sur hearthis ↗
Me and Funky Corners go way back. Ever since I find his show I didn't miss a single episode. He was the first one to host a la fabrock guest mix (episode 178) and 11 more after that. Todd started Funky Corners in 2008 and now has 328 shows under his belt. He was kind enough to contribute to the Funky Explorations series that Gelale and I started. Listen to Funky Corners for your weekly dose of dope old school hip-hop, funk, soul, the crate highlight and Todd's vibrant personnality and erudition. For this show, I'm happy to bring you the dark side of Todd, where you'll hear him pick songs by St Lucia, Underworld, Mick Ronson, Ini Kamoze and Mollusk. https://www.mixcloud.com/FunkyCorners More Topspin : https://www.mixcloud.com/lafabrock/playlists/topspin-musical-ping-pong-with-friends/ Une production by les tontons : facebook.com/tontontransistor As seen on radio 666 radio666.com (http://http://radio666.com/) and Raptz (http://www.raptz.com/)
