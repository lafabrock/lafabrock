Delitiae Musicae 4 (Classical meets Modern) with Magda Brand

En ligne le 15 novembre 201962 min20 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans l'archiveÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗Écouter sur hearthis ↗
Fab says: Classical is the new black. With these series Magda and I aim to shed a new light on classical music, explore the collision of old and new, the friction of modernity and tradition, offer a path to explore new takes on classical pieces, study how new artists deconstruct and reinvent, blur the lines, pay tribute to their forefathers and ultimately make the music of today with the sounds of yesterday and vice versa. Magda Says : Mozart said once: "there is nothing that music cannot do, as long as it remains music". I think I understood the meaning of those words the first time I heard Emerson, Lake and Palmer's "Pictures at an Exhibition". Contemporary classical music has much to offer: that's a good reason to feel excited about the big potential of this series, which Fab and myself curate with deep love for the music and great respect for the artists. I hope we can enthrall you with our selections without borders.
