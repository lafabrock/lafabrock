Funky Corners Show #192 Featuring LaFabrock 11-07-2015

Diffusée le 7 novembre 2015en ligne le 26 janvier 2016120 min48 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans l'archiveÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗
LaFabrock joins the program, once again, and delivers a raw DJ set which nearly out-Funky-Corners Funky Corners. He throws down some classics including Big Daddy Kane, Jungle Brothers, and Funkadelic. The Crate Highlight goes back to ’85 in Show 192. Including 3rd Bass, we drop five tracks which sampled the soul-influenced “Sledgehammer” by Peter Gabriel. Selections include: BeNeVoLeNcE, Bey Battery and Pruven, Capital Tax, Chill Rob G, Lyn Collins, D-Nice, DJ Shadow, Grant Green, Humble Beast, James Taylor Quartet, Jungle Brothers, Run The Jewels, RW & P38, Shabazz Palaces, Silento, Slim Jesus, TOK, Vinyl Dogs, and Booba.
