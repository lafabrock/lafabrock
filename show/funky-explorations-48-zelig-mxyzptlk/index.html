Funky Explorations #48 (Zelig Mxyzptlk)

En ligne le 4 novembre 201960 min15 morceaux

Gelale ( http://mixcloud.com/Gelale ) and I proudly re-welcome Zelig Mxyzptlk to our Funky Explorations. He was the star of episode 6 released 2 years ago and he is back for another run. Since that show, he published more than 120 episodes on Mixcloud and came up with new concepts to share the music he loves ("Around Midnight" and "Jazz") while keeping the funk alive on his "Is It Friday ?" and "Jazz Funk" series, vibing more than ever on Ness Radio. Some believe he is from Mexico, some say he only exists in the form of sound waves. For us he delivers once again his vision of Funk from the root to the fruit and we thank him for that. <3 <3 <3 : https://www.mixcloud.com/ZeligZelig/ Gelale pleases the crowd for the first 20 minutes Zelig has a goodtime ride for the next 20 minutes La fabrock calls the cops for the last 20 minutes Next episode: Funkologie (Switzerland) As heard on radio666.com and raptz.com
