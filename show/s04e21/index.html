S04e21 | Glass Island | 30th march 2017

To conclude this deep deep dive into the best new electro releases of the last few months (the last 5 shows) here’s a special episode featuring all the downtempo, chilled and atmospheric productions I’ve gathered. Contains music by Mirko, Philip Glass remixed, Bonobo, Superpoze, PYUR, Loscil and 2 extracts from a fantastic release by Frederic Robinson from October 2016 entitled Flea Waltz “a thrilling whirlpool of unexpected sonics, arrangements and themes, effortlessly picking up the vocabularies of jazz, world music, modern classical drum & bass” hospitalrecords.com/shop/relea...ic63-flea-waltz Hosted on Radio 666 http://www.radio666.com/ and http://www.rapporteuz.fr/ Join on facebook : https://www.facebook.com/lafabrock/ YouTube Channel : https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCd1uDHHQK28yFZn4ImBNW4w Soundcloud playlist : https://soundcloud.com/lafabrock/sets/s04e21-glass-island-30th-march
