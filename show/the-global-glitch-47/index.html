Gooood morning London! Welcome to the GLOBAL GLITCH show with MADERA VERDE on Mi SOUL RADIO, now DAB across the London area. Broadcasting weekly 5am to 7am every Sunday morning, for all the late night raving crew OR early start weekend warriors, its time to get glitchy ... First broadcast live on Mi Soul on Sunday 20 March 2016. Featuring a kaleidoscopic mix from French radio broadcaster LA FABROCK, broadcasting weekly out of Radio 666 in Caen, France. Also on the show, new ANCHORSONG, ELZA SOARES & DAYME AROCENA, classic LOOSE JOINTS, DENNIS ALCAPONE & SHABBA RANKS, the Sunrise Selection and the Paradise Garage Moment. Go on have a listen!
