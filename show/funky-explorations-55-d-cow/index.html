Funky Explorations #55 (D-Cow)

En ligne le 12 mai 202060 min19 morceaux

Gelale ( http://mixcloud.com/Gelale ) and I are most honoured to welcome D-Cow to your favourite Funk series. Finest austalian crate digger, with an inclination to food and ITs, D-Cow curates the most delightful mixcloud page and is affiliated to our beloved Ozi crew The Forty Five Kings. For us he delivers the ultimate funky mixtape that will make you forget all your troubles and dance the night away from crazy old gems to infectious southern tunes. It's mighty infectious and Gelale and I dig deeper in our crates to play along. More boundless funkyness : https://www.mixcloud.com/dave-cowan2/ Next Up : it's the return of the T-Wheels AKA Funky Corners! D-Cow pours some skunk juice during the first 20 minutes La fabrock makes it even flyer for the next 20 minutes Gelale invites your to a soul safari through the last 20 minutes As heard on Radio 666 and Raptz
