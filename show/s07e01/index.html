What better way to start season 7 than with a furious show full of nervous and abrasive cuts from my favorite recent metal releases ? This episode opens with Japanese female progressive punk act Otoboke Beaver, leans into some indie rock with Rema Rema, Snapped Ankles and King Gizzard and then fully embraces its dark side with Children Of Bodom, Steaksauce Mustache, Fallujah, In Flames and Killswitch Engage. In one word : #brace. We're back next thursday with some Topspin collaboration with my man Attila ! Be sure to check in ! As heard on Radio 666 and Radio Raptz
