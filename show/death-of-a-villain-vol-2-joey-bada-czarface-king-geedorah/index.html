Volumme 2 of 3 of our MF DOOM tribute mix. Daniel Dumile, AKA DOOM, METAL FACE or MF DOOM (All caps when you spell the name). Often described as "your favorite rappers favorite rapper", he leaves behind an impressive discography. Appart from the seminal album Madvillainy, in collaboration with Madlib, he will be remembered for his collaborations with a wide array of producers and rappers including Danger Mouse (DANGERDOOM), Jneiro Jarel (JJ DOOM), Czarface, Bishop Nerhu, Ghostface but also Gorillaz, Radiohead's Thom Yorke, J Dilla, The Avalanches, Tuxedo or BADBADNOTGOOD. This series of mixes humbly compile my favourite joints and celebrate the power of his rhyming, his mad producing skills, and the genius of his musical choices. Whether you are new to MF Doom or a long time fan, I hope you enjoy this retrospective.
