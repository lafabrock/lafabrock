Gelale ( http://mixcloud.com/Gelale ) and I proudly welcome MauroVecchi to our Funky Explorations. From Orvieto, Italy, comes yet another member of the Raptz Family ! For us he delivers his essential funky 20 minutes mix featuring that blends funk, soul, blues and rnb with a touch of Italian spices, a dash of few fancy new remixes. And you know us, we love to play along ! So come celebrate the end of summer with us, you know the word is groove ! Mauro Vecchi was infected with the funky syndrome at an early age. He spins house, disco, funk and groovy music on U-FM Radio in Rome for the Uptown Disco Sessions and delivers a weekly show for Raptz every week. More groovy music: https://www.mixcloud.com/djmaurovecchi/ Gelale funks the best for the first 20 minutes La fabrock blames it on his juice for the next 20 minutes Mauro Vecchi makes it hot and sweaty for the last 20 minutes Next episode: The Captain Who Went To Lunch (Serbia) As heard on radio666.com and raptz.com
