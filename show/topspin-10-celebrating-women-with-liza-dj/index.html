Topspin #10 | Celebrating Women with Liza DJ

En ligne le 14 novembre 201854 min18 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗Écouter sur hearthis ↗
Back in early 2018 when I discovered Liza's skills, I was impressed : simple, slick, effective, tasteful, powerful. A couple of months later I was launching collabs on the TopSpin project and Liza started her Rocking Women series to celebrate women's creativity. I had to invite her over for a musical ping pong. The result is this stunning collection of strong women anthems, a grateful ode to women from yesterday and today who redefined, inspired and fought for empowerment instead of expecting wishes and flowers. More Rocking Women : https://www.mixcloud.com/Liza_Dj/rockin-women-vol1/ More Topspin : https://www.mixcloud.com/lafabrock/playlists/topspin-musical-ping-pong-with-friends/ Une production by les tontons : facebook.com/tontontransistor Music by : Marilyn Monroe, Ute Lemper, Brigitte Bardot, M.I.A., Peaches, Lykke Li, Pussy Riot, Janelle Monáe, Erykah Badu, Beyoncé, Esma Redzepova, Eartha Kitt and more.
