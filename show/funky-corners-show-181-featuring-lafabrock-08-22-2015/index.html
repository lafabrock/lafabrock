It’s the return of the funk, and more importantly, the return of LaFabrock. He doubles his last output, and turns over a 30 minute set for the good of the Funky Corners cause. It’s a “Bad Tune” for the Crate Highlight. We play six tracks that sampled the 1971 Earth, Wind & Fire effort. Selections include: Air, Beastie Boys, Brand Nubian, Criolo, DJ Magic Mike, DJ Shadow, Fatback Band, Funky Bijou, Galaxy, Ghostface Killah, J Dilla, The Latin Kings, MF Doom, Method Man, The Pazant Brothers, Samba Soul, Seuil & Le Loup, Soul President, Melvin Sparks, Y Society, and Ice-T.
