Here's a new little series that might or might not become a thang : Looking Back At. We pick a year and try to come up with our favorite cuts from that year. It's 2024 so it's only fair that we look back at 1994. And who better than Todd Wheels AKA @FunkyCorners to go back in time with. We took to WhatsApp to elaborate a list of 20 songs. Ended up with 31. The rest is all about crafting our definitive 1994 mix featuring those songs... and maybe more. Here's mine. Yes it's 1 hour and 48 minutes long. But what a trip ! Hope you dig. Thank you T. Wheels
