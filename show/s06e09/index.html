S06e09 | SOUL | Toro y Moi, Delvon Lamarr Organ Trio, , Lakeside, Jimmy Smith, Macy Gray, Eli 'Paper

Les tontons and I are secretely working on our year end mix but for now, it's time for some Soul and Funk. 1. an exquisite Curtis Mayfiel cover with Delvon Lamarr (incredible Organ solo inside) 2. more organ sweetness by Jimmy Smith from a Live At The Florida Keys 1999 released early 2018. 3. new Toro y Moi : the return of the elastic bass. 4. good old Lakeside, 5. an underated Michael Jackson 6. Boogaloo and Latin infused Funk, 7. new Macy Gray and new Eli "Paperboy" Reed that sound retro and new at the same time 8. Joe Tex, Jerry Butler and The O'Jays bring some sweetness 9. classic break by Dave Cortez & The Moon People and Chamber Brothers used by Fatboy Slim 10. the opening from the latest "Late Night Tales" compiled by Agnes Obel : she made some outstanding choices including Henri Mancini "Evil Theme" from The Pink Panther Strikes Again (OST). 11. some seasonal surprise. Best wishes. Enjoy. As heard on http://http://radio666.com/ and http://www.raptz.com/
