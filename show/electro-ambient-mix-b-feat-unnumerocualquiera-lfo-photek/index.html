Electro Ambient Mix B | feat. UnNumeroCualquiera | LFO, Photek, The Prodigy, Dj Shadow, Dj Spooky...

En ligne le 18 mars 201984 min36 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗Écouter sur hearthis ↗
This is the second part of my electro ambient trip with UnNumeroCualquiera https://www.mixcloud.com/unnumerocualquiera/ The man from Madrid sent a wild selection of 90's electro and ambient as he only could do. The truth is I don't have so much 90's electro in store (LFO, Photek), so I included those new artists that still emulate the 90's sound (Helena Hauff, Blawan, Lee Gamble, DMX Krew). I've used early ambient sounds (Harmonia 76, LFO) as well as their latest counterparts (Black To Comm AKA Max Richter, Mondkopf, Johannsson) I've included personal favorites from the 90's (Daft Punk, Nine Inch Nails) as well as music I heard in the OST of Climax by Gaspar Noé (Dopplereffekt). Amon Tobin and Tobacco mirror the Dj Shadow remixes that UNC selected. The Prodigy is there because they were a big deal in the 90's and Keith Flint just passed away. Listen to part A : http://bit.ly/lfbrckunca As heard on http://http://radio666.com/ and http://www.raptz.com/
