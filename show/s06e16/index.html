De retour avec une sélection haute en couleur, riche en saveur qui va vous réchauffer, vous faire sourire et danser. _*_*_*_*_ Back at you with a warm selection of brand new tunes that will make smile and dance. This episode bears the sun in its heart and delivers : * the positive message of Youssou N'Dour * the sounds of afrobeat from Baloji and Khalifa * the soul power of James Brown and The Soul Motivators * the playful combination of Steve Lacy and Vampire Weekend * Cumbia sweetness from Grupo Jejeje * Modern Afrocumbia hybrids dug up by Hawaii Bonsaii Records * Salsa thrills with Daniela Darcourt * The Turkish delights of Altin Gün * The dancefloor appeal of mashups, remixes, and edits by Manati, Karnival, Borchi or Barbarella) Wishing you the best month of June, under the sun, feets in the grass, the sand or trapped at the office, in your car, La Fabrock delivers.
