So happy to share this batch of new electro releases with you. From the nasty angry techno beats of Ivy Lab (UK), Dj Snake (FR) or Digitalism (DE) we sail on the verge of experimental techno and sweetly fall in the libms of new releases from Daneil Avery(UK), Aphex Twin (UK), Squarepusher (UK) or Arca (Venezuela)'s IDM. Caribou (Canada), Rejoicer(UK), Four tet (UK) and legendary Sasha (UK) also contribute to blurring the line between dance music and building the weirdest and most exciting sounds of these last few months. Enjoy the sounds of La fabrock on Radio 666 and Raptz.
