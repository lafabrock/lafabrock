Delitiae Musicae

Delitiae Musicae 3 (Classical meets Modern) with Magda Brand

En ligne le 11 avril 201960 min20 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗Écouter sur hearthis ↗
There's nothing quite like making music with the people you love. Especially when they are as kind and gifted with the sweetest musical taste as Magda Brand. For us Mixcloud people, making music together means sending over bits of mixes that we can thread on and craft to reflect our moods and dig on topics we chose to investigate. Magda and I share the opinion that there isn't enough room for modern classical music on Mixcloud (please prove us wrong) so we decided to work on those Delitiae Musicae mixes where we explore the friction points between classical and modern music. In this mix, you'll hear piano, guitar, strings, oboe, accordion, voices, MPCs and all the notes that dwell in our hearts and ears within the realms of Jazz, Soul, Classical, Rap, Ambient and more. We sincerely hope you enjoy this as much as we do. As heard on radio666 and raptz More Delitiae Musicae : http://bit.ly/delitiaemusicae More Magda : http://bit.ly/mxcldmagda Photo by Federico Beccari
