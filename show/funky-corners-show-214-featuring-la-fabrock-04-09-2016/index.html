Funky Corners Show #214 Featuring La Fabrock 04-09-2016

Diffusée le 9 avril 2016en ligne le 15 avril 2016120 min43 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗
We roll up a bit of ragga hip hop to ignite Funky Corners #214. La Fabrock breezes through with another extraordinary guest set, and we top off the program with some grimy NY rap. Find out which jazz track was sampled by De La, Bored Stiff, Da King & I, and Action Bronson, half-way though the show. Selections include: Bad Medicine, Brian Bennett, D.A. Jay, DJ 2-Tone Jones, DJ Bigg H, DJ Paypal, DJ Vladim, Finsta Bundy, Freddie Foxxx, Fugees, The Game, Gang Starr, Jay-Z, Mad Lion, Damian Marley, Bruno Nicolai, Richie Branson & Solar Slim, Suff Daddy, U.S. Girls, Vald, Boris Vian, Saul Williams, Adrian Younge, and Bass Mekanik.
