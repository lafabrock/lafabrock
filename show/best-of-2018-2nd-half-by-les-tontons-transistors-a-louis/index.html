Vous êtes sur le point d'écouter notre best of du 2nd semestre 2018. 60 chansons mixées dans 3 épisodes palpitants. Aujourd'hui épisode A. ____________________ Brace ! It's that time of the year when Les Tontons Transistors bring you their thrilling year end mix. We selected 60 songs released in the second half of 2018 and crafted 3x 1 hour mixes with them. Today, part. A. Be sure to come back next week for part B! What's your favourite music in 2018? Let us know in comment section. Thanks for all the love!
