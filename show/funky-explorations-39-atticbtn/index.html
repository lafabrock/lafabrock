Gelale ( http://mixcloud.com/Gelale ) and I are proud to welcome ATTICBTN A.K.A Ovre to this new episode of our funky explorations. From Brighton, UK, they are a DJ Duo that generally play more house, electro, Italo but accepted to deliver their vision of Funk for you. They hold a residency at Brighton club ‘Patterns’ but also run ‘ATTIC’, a party that covers a wide variety of sounds Dance the night away: http://mixcloud.com/ATTICBTN Gelale brings all good things for the first 20 minutes La fabrock strikes a big blow through the next 20 minutes ATTICBTN brings some love for the last 20 minutes Next episode: DJ F@SOUL (Austria) As heard on radio666.com and raptz.com
