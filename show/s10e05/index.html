S10e05 | Jupiter In Retrograde

En ligne le 30 novembre 202260 min25 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗
11 years ago, before this show even started, I was living in China, I played in a few bars as a DJ and I would craft a lot of mixes for friends or parties. The first 10 minutes of this mix is a re-edition of a mix I used to love. At this point, I don't know where this show is moving - or if it's even going to last at all. Maybe I'll just pause it, maybe I'll delete all the shows and rejoice of how low my digital carbon footprint is hahaha. Thank you Mixcloud followers, collaborators, broadcasters and friends. It's been a lot of fun. And music geekitude.
