This show dates back from early 2024. It was only release on August 24 because I feel quite conflicted about feeding the cloud monster and I want to focus on other things. In the end, the main reason why I do this show is for the pleasure of crafting mixes so I’ll stick to it and not worry too much about advertising it. Love and respect for those who do. If this mix reaches you and you like It than that we share something truly unique and that makes me happy. Take care, do the rock 🤘 and eat some carrots 🥕
