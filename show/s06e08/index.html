With this opening by Mick Jenkins paying tribute to Gill Scott Heron, you know you can't go wrong. This show brings to you new material from the most exciting rappers on the scene right now (Odisee, Vince Staples, YG, Nas, Action Bronson) as well as some incredible collaborations : A$AP Rocky meets french techno beatmaker Gesaffelstein, Swizz Beats recruits NAS, Homeboy Sandman joins forces with Edan. You will also enjoy some cooler cuts by Jungle, NoName, Bacao Rhythm & Steel Band and King Krule. Older material includes a Clipse remix, a Nas remix. Most notably, Swamp Dogg delivers an incredibly weird auto-tuny song celebrating sex with exes in the most candid way. Enjoy. As heard on http://http://radio666.com/ and http://www.raptz.com/
