Gelale ( https://www.mixcloud.com/Gelale ) and I are very happy to bring another Canadian contributor to our Funky Explorations series with the surprising Esh from We Need More Crates Radio. Esh is a MPC lover, a former hip-hop producer who made a reputation on the Torronto Hip-Hop scene and the kind of people who cares about records, especially what's on the flipside of a record - we're talking strictly vinyl here right !? He's been collecting records for 20 years but his radio show "We Need More Crates" is only 69 episodes young and already featured on Radio Raptz. Check out his rich hip-hop, funk, soul, reggae and jazz selections on https://www.mixcloud.com/marc-eshelman/ Gelale starts and crashes the party avec une bonne dose de boogie from Paris for the first 20 minutes Esh brings some scratches and reverb through the next 20 minutes La fabrock does it to deff for the last 20 minutes Next episode: Alex from Le Petit Bazar Electro (France)
