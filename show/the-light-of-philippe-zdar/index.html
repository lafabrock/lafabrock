Hommage

The light of Philippe Zdar

En ligne le 8 juillet 201976 min30 morceaux
▶ Écouter et explorer dans les archivesÉcouter sur Mixcloud ↗Écouter sur hearthis ↗
My love for Philippe Zdar has always been real. He has been the sunshine in many lives. Reading about the words his friends had for him after he passed away inspired me to put this together. Wether as a writer, producer, remixer or engineer, Philippe Zdar leaves behind an inspiring discography. From his early days (no speed metal though) to his latest collaborations, most of his friends are here : Thomas Bangalter, the big brother Dimitri who got him his nickname Zdar Mehdi who introduced him to Chromeo Pharrell Williams MC Solaar, The Beastie Boys, Phoenix, Rapture, Gossip, Jackson, Sebastien Tellier Etienne De Crecy (Motorbass or La Chatte Rouge) Cat Power with whom he shared a deep connection Dj Falcon, Breakbot, Ed Rec crew and Pedro Winter Hubert (Cassius and La Funk Mob) Before I die, I'll sure visit the Motorbass studio. In the meantime, Let me remind you of the grace of his deepest love. Rest in Peace, Philippe we love you so.
